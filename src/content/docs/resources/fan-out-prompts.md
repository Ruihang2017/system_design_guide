## Prompts to fan out

Each module can become its own deep-dive. These are prompts you can bring to an AI assistant (e.g., Claude) or use as self-study deep-dive targets:

- "Expand **Module 4 (databases)** into a deep dive with a decision tree for choosing a database from access patterns, and concrete examples."
- "Do a deep dive on **consistency and replication** with diagrams of leader-follower, multi-leader, and quorum reads."
- "Walk me through the **caching module** with real cache-stampede mitigation code and a worked invalidation example."
- "Fully work the **Chat / WhatsApp** case study end-to-end using the framework, like the two worked examples here."
- "Give me a **flashcard set** (concept → tradeoff) for Modules 0–6 so I can self-test."
- "Turn the **Part 11 framework** into a one-page checklist I can keep open during interviews."
- "Quiz me: throw a random system design prompt and critique my approach as I go."
