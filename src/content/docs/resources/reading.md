## Canonical resources

- ***Designing Data-Intensive Applications* by Martin Kleppmann** — the single best book for the data/distributed-systems side of everything in Modules 1, 2, 4, and 6. If you read one thing, read this.
- ***System Design Interview* (Vol 1 & 2) by Alex Xu** — practical, case-study-driven, matched closely to interview format.
- **The System Design Primer** (open-source GitHub repo by Donne Martin) — a free, comprehensive companion to this guide.
- **Grokking the Modern System Design Interview** (Educative) — structured interactive course.
- **Company engineering blogs** — Netflix, Uber, Discord, Stripe, Cloudflare. Real systems, real tradeoffs; the best "advanced" material once fundamentals are solid.
- **ByteByteGo** (Alex Xu) — short, visual explanations of individual concepts; good for spaced review.
